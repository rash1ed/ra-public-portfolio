# Career Operations OS — Auditable Recruiting Operations

## Business problem

High-volume job search becomes unreliable when vacancies, email threads, CV versions, portals and follow-ups live in separate places. Activity can look high while the actual outcome remains unclear.

## Operating model

The Career Operations OS converts that fragmented flow into one governed funnel:

**Discover → Verify → Score → Route → Execute → Confirm → Follow up → Classify outcome**

Core controls include:
- Canonical application ledger
- Employer and company watchlists
- Opportunity scoring and mandatory-fit gates
- Recipient-quality registry
- CV-route controls
- Follow-up queue
- Portal execution queue and execution evidence
- Career event log
- Conversion analytics by source and role family

## Evidence model

A task is not considered complete because an email was drafted or a portal button was clicked.

The system distinguishes:
- opportunity discovered;
- vacancy verified;
- route approved;
- send / submission attempted;
- external evidence confirmed;
- employer response classified;
- downstream interview / assessment / offer outcome.

## Control logic

Scoring or routing changes are designed to move through:
1. simulation;
2. shadow evaluation;
3. limited canary;
4. readback;
5. production promotion.

This prevents a new rule or model from silently changing the whole application funnel.

## Business translation

The same pattern applies to corporate operations: one source of truth, clear workflow states, controlled handoffs, exception queues and evidence-based reporting.
